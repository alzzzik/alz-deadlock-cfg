# Quiet Valve builds leave one real particle fix

From 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-19 Valve shipped five builds and changed **nothing you can tune**. The one change in the window that matters for your files came from the community: **`r_particle_min_timestep "0.00241"` makes fast particles look jagged above about 415 FPS**, and both of your `gameinfo.gi` files carry it. Both of your `video.txt` files set `"setting.fps_max" "0"`. `video.txt` wins over `gameinfo.gi` for menu-owned settings, so the `fps_max "400"` line in `gameinfo.gi` caps nothing and your game runs uncapped. That means you can hit the bug in quiet moments, so **comment the line out in both files**. Everything else is minor. Your explicit `Mod`/`Write` search-path lines are correct: the documented cause of the `user_keys_default` crash is a gameinfo that has lost them, so keep them. Do not copy OptiLock's new "Grimoire" `FileSystem` block, because it deletes Valve's `UserSettingsPathID` lines. Deadlock Mod Manager (DMM) no longer rewrites a `gameinfo.gi` whose search paths already match its own. Sqooky's 3.6 is mostly HUD and minimap changes, and player reports in the window are about crashes after the new heroes, not FPS gains. Nobody published a measured FPS or frame-time number for any setting in the window. Every gain below is still something you have to confirm with your own A/B tests on your 5700X.

## Five Valve builds left the tunable surface untouched

Valve shipped builds **6782 (10-14), 6790 (10-16, the Violet hero update), 6793, 6794, 6795 and 6796 (10-19)**. Only 6790 touched `convars.txt`. It added two `developmentonly` HUD convars (`citadel_damage_text_follow_healthbar_scale` and `citadel_hud_high_unspent_souls_threshold`) and changed one description. It removed nothing, changed no flags and changed no defaults. The `gameinfo_cannot_override` count stays at 124 and the `r_citadel_*` count at 73 ([GameTracking 9b616d3](https://github.com/SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Deadlock/commit/9b616d3093a7f3e25e852ec93c9b818c5d2c94ed)). Stock `gameinfo.gi` is **byte-identical to the 6779 file**, so the `PGIVersion "39A735A4…"` in both of your files still matches stock ([GT gameinfo.gi history](https://github.com/SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Deadlock/commits/master/game/citadel/gameinfo.gi)). The matchmaking guard text still lists the same seven sections (Engine2, MaterialSystem2, NetworkSystem, Particles, RenderSystem, SceneSystem, WorldRenderer). `FileSystem` and `ConVars` are not on that list. The video menu (`popup_settings.xml`) only gained a ping-wheel localization token, so `video.txt` Version 20 is still current ([9b616d3](https://github.com/SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Deadlock/commit/9b616d3093a7f3e25e852ec93c9b818c5d2c94ed)).

The research checked every `ConVars` line in your stable `gameinfo.gi` (255 lines) and experimental `gameinfo.gi` (281 lines), and every `setting.*` key in both `video.txt` files (57 each), against the 6779 and 6796 dumps. Every check returned **identical results**. The same five names were blocked before the window and still are: `lb_enable_dynamic_lights`, `lb_enable_baked_shadows`, `lb_enable_stationary_lights`, `sc_fade_distance_scale_override` and `r_citadel_gpu_culling` ([GT convars.txt @6796](https://github.com/SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Deadlock/blob/2e024f9/DumpSource2/convars.txt)). Valve did not deliver the "performance cvars" promised in OptimizationLock #88. The thread has had no new Valve-relayed statement since 10-14 ([OptimizationLock #88](https://github.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock/issues/88)). One detail favours a line your experimental file already has: 6790 shipped **eight new Violet particle-light effects** (`artist_*_light.vpcf_c`). These are exactly the lights that `r_citadel_disable_particle_lights` suppresses, so the convar should be worth a little more in fights with Violet ([9b616d3 pak01_dir.txt](https://github.com/SteamDatabase/GameTracking-Deadlock/commit/9b616d3093a7f3e25e852ec93c9b818c5d2c94ed)). That is an inference from file names, not a measurement. Valve rebuilt the engine DLLs in 6782 and 6790. GameTracking cannot show code-level changes, so only a before/after benchmark could reveal a performance change from those rebuilds.

## Uncapped FPS turns the 415 Hz particle timestep into a visible bug

ghempy53 ran the only real experiment of the window. The owner saw "jagged" fire on Deadman Danny's jetpack and on heal particles, and bisected six rounds down to a single line. Full-resolution textures didn't fix it. Removing the `r_particle_max_texture_layers 4` cap "looked worse". Resetting HDR alone didn't fix it. Resetting **only `r_particle_min_timestep` to Valve's default of 0** did. Valve's help text explains why: "particle simulation happening more frequently than this will lerp". The value 0.00241 s works out to **about 415 Hz**, so above roughly 415 FPS particles are interpolated between simulation steps instead of being simulated every frame ([ghempy53 edcae9e](https://github.com/ghempy53/deadlock-config/commit/edcae9e); [TESTED-CHANGES.md](https://github.com/ghempy53/deadlock-config/blob/main/docs/TESTED-CHANGES.md)). The evidence is the owner's visual check, not a capture, but the mechanism matches Valve's own description.

The research note that collected this finding rated your exposure as low because of `fps_max "400"`. That rating is wrong for your setup. Both of your `video.txt` files set `"setting.fps_max" "0"`, and `video.txt` wins, so **your game runs uncapped**. A 5700X easily passes 415 FPS in lanes, the jungle and the hideout, which is exactly where the jetpack-fire symptom was seen. Removing the line costs nothing in the moments you care about. In CPU-bound fights your frame rate is well below 415, so the timestep never fires there anyway. Above 415 the extra particle simulation only spends headroom you already have. Sqooky's warning that lower values "could negatively impact performance" applies only to frame rates above 415, where you have frames to spare. **Verdict: comment out `r_particle_min_timestep` in both files.** ghempy53's stable did the same. Leave the `fps_max "400"` line marked as dead, or delete it, so the file stops implying a cap that doesn't exist. If you ever want a real cap, set it in the in-game menu or in `video.txt`.

## Big configs churned HUD lines and one risky FileSystem rewrite

Sqooky shipped **OptimizationLock 3.5.3 (10-16) and 3.6 / Max-FPS 1.5 (10-17)**. Neither release changes an engine section or any render or particle convar. 3.6 reverts `citadel_camera_use_vmdl_flatten_vertical` to `"true"`, the value you already run, after it broke the scope cameras of Rem, Venator and Rat King. The Rat King camera issue (#70) closed on 10-19. 3.6 also comments out `lb_enable_baked_shadows "false"`, which has been blocked since 6769 and is still in both of your files. The rest is taste: healthbar interpolation off (`healthbar_highlight_speed 999`), smaller trooper dots, and `minimap_trooper_update_rate_hz`, which is server-only. 3.6 also adds a launch-option header (`-particles 1 -softparticlesdefaultoff -particle_disable_diffuse -nojoy …`) with no evidence that these Source 1 era flags do anything in Deadlock ([commit aba20cd](https://github.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock/commit/aba20cd); [DMM catalog.json](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/blob/main/packages/perf-catalog/data/catalog.json)). The two big packs disagree on `citadel_per_weapon_per_surface_impact_effects`: OptiLock set it to `true` to fix the Yamato right-click radius, and Sqooky set it to `false` two days later. It is `replicated`, so the server's value may win anyway. Leave it out.

OptiLock rebased its presets onto the current stock file on 10-14. On 10-16 it added a **"Grimoire" `FileSystem` block**. That block wraps `SearchPaths` in DMM markers, adds `Game citadel/grimoire` and explicit `Mod`/`Write` lines, and **deletes `"UserSettingsPathID" "USRLOCAL"` and `"LegacyUserSettingsPathID" "MOD"`**. It also edits `ResourceCompiler` keys that only matter when compiling maps ([commit fca258f](https://github.com/dacooderr/OptiLock/commit/fca258f)). Do not copy it. Its one useful idea, explicit `Mod`/`Write` lines, is already in your files. The deleted lines are Valve stock, and they plausibly control where per-user settings and keybinds are read from. OptiLock's own keyboard bugs (keys swapping A↔D and S↔W followed by crashes, and SharpKeys remaps failing "when using the modified gameinfo.gi") were closed on 10-14 with no root cause stated ([OptiLock #12](https://github.com/dacooderr/OptiLock/issues/12); [#10](https://github.com/dacooderr/OptiLock/issues/10)). The `grimoire` path serves a different mod manager that you don't use.

Your 10-19 search-path fix stands. The `user_keys_default` fatal error ("Unable to read default keybinding configuration") has two documented causes: an outdated mod shipping its own `cfg/user_keys_default.vcfg`, or a `gameinfo.gi` that has lost the `Game citadel/addons` plus `Mod`/`Write` lines, so the engine looks for the file in the wrong place ([MCLauncher PR #8](https://github.com/camwooloo/MCLauncher/pull/8); [Frogtech guide](https://mods.frogtech.dev/guides/deadlock-mods-not-working-after-update)). Your block, `Game citadel/addons` → `Mod`/`Write citadel` → `Game citadel` → `Mod`/`Write core` → `Game core`, followed by both stock `UserSettingsPathID` lines, covers the second cause. `FileSystem` is outside the guarded sections, and Valve did not touch stock `SearchPaths` in the window. Keep the block exactly as it is. Two other community projects had no new evidence: compylock (liv's base) and deadtune had **no commits** in the window. dyson's GameBanana config grew to 67 engine-section edits on a pre-City-Never-Sleeps base, so it is not a source for a "looks decent" config ([DMM sources diff](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/commit/e3a8346)).

## DMM stopped rewriting unchanged files while players reported crashes, not gains

DMM made two changes that matter for you. Since **10-18 it skips rewriting `gameinfo.gi` when the search paths it would write are unchanged** ([commit 7616335](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/commit/7616335)). On 10-17 it learned to name a read-only `gameinfo.gi`, a setup some guides recommend, and offer to make it writable, and it now falls back to a validated backup when resetting to vanilla ([commit 40531b3](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/commit/40531b3)). The crash check is now on by default, and on 10-19 DMM added a mod bisect that "find[s] the mod that breaks the game by testing in halves" ([commit 7557bad](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/commit/7557bad); [commit d8d6e74](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/commit/d8d6e74)). One issue is still open: #875 reports custom FPS/FOV `gameinfo.gi` files being **wiped or failing to parse on modded launches**, with about 20 incidents around the 10-13 update and no maintainer reply yet ([#875](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/issues/875)). Keep a backup, keep the file cleanly formatted, and don't mark it read-only. The perf catalog refresh (10-18) added no presets. Its deny rule against `Engine2/RenderingPipeline/DistanceField 0` ("crashes the current game build after the hideout loads") is satisfied by your `DistanceField "1"` ([catalog.json](https://github.com/deadlock-mod-manager/deadlock-mod-manager/blob/main/packages/perf-catalog/data/catalog.json)).

Player reports in the window have **no measured FPS data**. They are all about breakage. One forum thread says the Solomon update "broke the fps config commands", but that comes from a search snippet and is unverified ([forum thread 174251](https://forums.playdeadlock.com/threads/solomon-update-broke-the-fps-config-commands.174251/)). OptimizationLock #98 reports crashes once Solomon or Violet load into a match on "the newest gameinfo", closed with no fix given ([#98](https://github.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock/issues/98)). In #89 and #90 Sqooky blamed launch failures and locked heroes on forgotten outdated mods, and the reporter confirmed it ([#90](https://github.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock/issues/90)). Issue #93 lists visual bugs from aggressive packs: missing orb text, black hero silhouettes, a misspelled language path and a short far-clip plane ([#93](https://github.com/Sqooky/OptimizationLock/issues/93)). Your files pass that checklist. `r_farz` is `-1`, the default. The language path is `citadel_*LANGUAGE*`. There are no panel-DPI overrides and no `fog_enable false`. Nobody published a player report or FPS number for `r_citadel_disable_particle_lights` or `citadel_player_model_force_highest_lod`; a web search for the first returned no Deadlock results at all. Kaizu's config produced "repeating" light patches on floors (#86, closed 10-18), and no report ties that to particle lights. On drivers, **617.42 (10-06) is still NVIDIA's current Game Ready driver**, with no Deadlock issue listed. October is the last month of Game Ready drivers for Windows 10 ([TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/352020/final-nvidia-geforce-game-ready-driver-for-windows-10-goes-out-in-october)). Nobody reported a ban or a new matchmaking block. A status site lists a "Not all party members…" matchmaking error added 10-16, but that comes from a snippet and is unverified ([isthisgamedown](https://isthisgamedown.com/deadlock/)).

## Conclusion

The window shows that the engine side has stabilised. Valve's tunable surface is frozen at 6779, so the remaining work on your config is correctness, not new levers. The one real find in five days was an interaction between layers, not a new convar. A gameinfo line that looks harmless next to `fps_max 400` becomes a visible bug once `video.txt` uncaps the game. That is the strongest argument for treating `gameinfo.gi` and `video.txt` as one system and auditing which layer actually wins for each setting. It also shows that the community's "FPS" lines can make the game look worse on fast CPUs, the opposite of their purpose. The most likely next change is a convar diff with the next hero build (a 10-20 hero release is rumoured but unverified). Check that diff for `release`-flagged `r_citadel_*` or `cl_particle_*` additions, the promised performance convars. Until then, the only FPS evidence that should promote a line is your own in-match A/B.

**What to test in game**

Test in this order. Turn on `cl_hud_telemetry_frametime_show 1` and run each test in a real match where you can.

1. **Particles.** After removing the timestep line, stand in a lane or the hideout at more than 415 FPS. Watch Deadman Danny's jetpack fire and heal effects for jaggedness. Then check that fight frame times look the same as before.
2. **Particle lights.** Toggle `r_citadel_disable_particle_lights 1` / `0` live in the same fight, ideally one with Violet. Watch frame times and look for repeating light patches on floors.
3. **Keybinds.** Launch once **without** DMM and confirm there is no `user_keys_default` fatal error. Change a keybind, restart, and confirm it persisted.
4. **DMM.** Launch once **with** DMM. Then diff `gameinfo.gi` against your copy. If DMM rewrote it, its generated block differs from yours. Keep the backup and don't set the file to read-only.
5. **Crashes.** If you crash only in matches with Solomon or Violet, first disable addons or run DMM's mod bisect. Only then suspect the cfg by testing stock `gameinfo.gi`.
6. **Party queue.** Queue once in a party with the modded cfg, to rule out the unverified 10-16 "Not all party members" error.
